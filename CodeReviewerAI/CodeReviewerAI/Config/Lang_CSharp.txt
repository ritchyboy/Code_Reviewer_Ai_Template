### Language Strategy: C# (.NET Core / Unity)

**Primary Focus:** Modern C# and .NET Standards.

**Review Checklist:**
1.  **Architecture:**
    * Violations of SOLID, DRY, and KISS.
    * Dependency Injection usage.
2.  **C# Best Practices:**
    * Naming conventions (PascalCase for methods, camelCase for locals).
    * Proper use of LINQ vs Loops.
3.  **Async/Await:**
    * Flag any blocking calls like `.Result` or `.Wait()`.
    * Ensure `await` is used all the way up the stack.
4.  **Security:**
    * **CRITICAL:** Redact secrets/passwords.
    * Check for SQL Injection risks.
